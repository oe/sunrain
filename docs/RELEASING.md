# Releasing Sunrain

Sunrain is a static education, symptom-reference and self-care site. A passing software build does not establish clinical validity. The exact boundary of the source-backed reference edition is recorded in [SCREENING_IMPLEMENTATION.md](./SCREENING_IMPLEMENTATION.md).

## Current release decision

The 2026-10-19 release is a general mental-health education, self-care and help-navigation website. It is not a professional mental-health service, diagnostic tool or treatment programme. The project owner authorised publication within this scope; hiring clinicians in every supported country is not a prerequisite for this edition.

The previous general-support release kept scoring closed. The proposed symptom-reference edition replaces that blanket policy with exact source/rights/version/locale admission, strict scoring completeness, adult/youth separation and explicit unassessed states. Its requirements include complete eight-language support paths and scope/privacy statements, honest unreviewed-content status, working stop/help paths and passing software checks. AI source and language-consistency checks do not become clinical or native-speaker approval. Independent expert review, real-user research and physical-device testing remain useful future work; their absence is recorded rather than presented as completed or used to indefinitely hold this general-support edition.

## Supported scope

- Eight interface languages, including Arabic RTL, with no English fallback in the support content pack.
- One private check-in URL offers primary symptom screening and separate general reflection. PHQ-9 is available in eight languages; GAD-7 in seven, with Japanese GAD-7 explicitly unavailable because of a translation restriction; Japanese instead offers the author-permitted six-item K6 broad-distress record (30 days, 0–24, no diagnostic cutoffs or severity bands). Completed tools receive separate results, skipped/unselected/unavailable tools stay unassessed. PHQ/GAD adult symptom-reference bands and youth raw-score records have separate handling; K6 remains a raw record at either age, and its 30-day recall is kept distinct from PHQ-9’s two weeks. Related coping activities can appear together; no combined score, disease probability or diagnosis is generated.
- General support has no 18+ confirmation or age collection. All eight locales welcome younger visitors, explain the reference-only scope and encourage early professional help or support from a safe trusted adult/youth service. Parent permission is not a site-access prerequisite; medical-service consent and eligibility depend on the actual provider. NHS youth guidance is available alongside the explicitly adult England talking-therapies entry, including without JavaScript. The symptom-reference branch asks only an optional age band: 13–17 receives a symptom record without adult severity bands; under 13 or declining an age answer retains the complete general-support and youth-help path. This does not claim youth clinical validation of the eight translations.
- Four general activities: grounding, a manageable step, a sleep routine and organising a worry. Each has optional everyday examples, four guided steps, an editable small plan, stopping, feedback and onward support. Sensory steps can be skipped, with this option explained before starting. These are not validated treatment programmes. Without JavaScript, every instruction remains readable.
- The recommended activity and help preparation can run inside the check-in page. The one-step activity carries the selected action into later steps and reflection, using the first existing optional note field. Manual edits are preserved; explicit note loading and clearing update the displayed action. Notes remain optional and are not loaded or saved automatically. Original answers, the small plan and feedback remain available when the user explicitly chooses to prepare contact. Direct activity pages also offer preparation on the same page. No cross-page storage or URL carries health information. Refresh, language changes, navigation away and return from browser history clear unsaved content.
- The worry activity additionally carries optional public concern/part choices through a review and completion page. It reuses the same three optional note fields; those choices add no stored fields. Users can skip, stop, edit or reset; a worse response retains care/crisis access. A proposed plan is never labelled as an action already completed. Source, localization and service-check limits are recorded in [worry-flow-review.json](./content/worry-flow-review.json).
- Each of the four illustrated topics includes one everyday situation, three practical steps and a visible next action. Optional sections explain difficulty/no benefit and provide an adaptable way to open a help conversation. The bipolar example prioritises professional assessment; no activity, questionnaire or relaxation routine is presented as a substitute. These examples introduce no form fields, storage or regional service claims. Source and localization limits are recorded in [topic-practice-review.json](./content/topic-practice-review.json).
- Editable contact text includes all nonempty preparation fields, with no unresolved sentence placeholders. Plans are labelled as considered support rather than completed treatment. A too-long activity record is explicitly announced; the complete plan remains in the page and can be downloaded separately. Copy, explicit device save/load/delete and local text download are available. Copy denial leaves the message and download available. An external link never sends the note.
- Official routine-care entry points for England, the US, Japan, South Korea, Spain and Hong Kong, plus one hospital's own booking instructions in Beijing. The Beijing entry is explicitly a single provider, not China-wide coverage. `src/data/support-services.json` is the runtime catalog; its entry-page checks are recorded in `docs/content/support-resources.json`. Other regions receive an explicit coverage-gap message and local primary-care guidance. Country is never inferred from language. External appointment booking, acceptance, waiting time and service-language availability are not verified by this site.
- England and Japan additionally have eight-language, optional practical guidance in `src/data/support-pathways.json`: eligibility/scope, three navigation steps, costs, care languages and contact/waiting limits. England's youth route remains visible. Entry-page dates are per listing and distinct from guidance checks. The resources collection has localized link labels, explicit English/adult labels and verified UCLA audio-language section links; external documents and audio are not translated, rehosted or clinically reviewed by Sunrain.
- Crisis support links to the maintained Find A Helpline directory. It is not emergency dispatch; immediate danger must go directly to local emergency services. If the directory is unavailable, the emergency path remains explicit and does not redirect to routine booking. The previous unverified phone/SMS list is retained only as source history, not rendered.

## Clinical and language gates

`src/data/support-copy.json` is the runtime content source; its review metadata is intentionally honest. Independent clinical and native-language review is pending. Original source references do not mean the source organisation approved Sunrain's wording or translations. The separate sourceConsistency/languageConsistency fields record an AI editorial check of source scope and high-consequence meaning across the eight locales; they do not change the independent professional or native-language review fields.

`src/data/screening-registry.json` pins the admitted source forms, regional variants, PDF and transcription digests, manual, rights evidence and honest review status. `screening-content.ts` refuses changed or unverified content; unverified locale text is never serialized. The old YAML candidates are not used. Existing assessment bookmarks describe availability and lead to the central private flow. Rights for the Japanese 2025 GAD-7 have not been obtained, so that version remains closed.

This source-backed **symptom-reference** edition implements the owner's request for meaningful initial screening without claiming a professional service or fabricated expert approval. The former universal qualified-review gate is replaced, for this limited edition, by original-form/manual fidelity, verified display rights, visible editorial limits and the technical checks described in the implementation record. Independent clinical/native review remains pending and visible. There is no custom diagnostic algorithm, treatment selection, bipolar diagnosis quiz, suicide prediction/risk grade or guarantee from a low score. Medical diagnosis, treatment-oriented additions, new instruments and new claims require a separate scope and evidence review; do not infer approval from the current reference edition.

After the general-support release, repository maintainers continue to check source changes and broken service links, correct wording and translations, and act on usability feedback. Gradually extend regional coverage only with traceable entry points and clear limits. Real-user research and device testing can improve confidence, but must not be described as clinical-outcome evidence.

## Reproducible validation

Use Node 24 (minimum 22.22) and the declared pnpm version through Corepack:

```sh
corepack pnpm install --frozen-lockfile
corepack pnpm audit --audit-level=high
corepack pnpm test:unit
corepack pnpm build
corepack pnpm verify:sunrain
corepack pnpm exec playwright install --with-deps chromium
corepack pnpm test:e2e
```

The build validates all eight locales, local links, private-page telemetry exclusions and exact screening availability, including the restricted Japanese translation. Daily CI runs one job with the complete desktop Chromium suite and thirteen selected mobile Chromium smoke cases. Desktop exercises all eight support flows, screening edge cases, keyboard operation, storage/clipboard denial, long-note preservation, no-JavaScript reading and the full audio regressions. Mobile repeats the core journeys, RTL keyboard controls, narrow layouts, worry-plan continuity and audio mixing controls.

Before a release or when changing browser-sensitive code, use the manual compatibility option described in [CI.md](./CI.md). It runs twelve support-flow smoke cases on desktop Firefox, desktop WebKit and mobile WebKit. Audio signal and lifecycle checks remain in Chromium; the optional suite does not certify Firefox/WebKit audio playback. Consult actual run results rather than treating a configured project as tested. The build fingerprint must match the checked-out source; do not edit files while building or testing. `PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE_PATH` can select an existing Chromium binary locally. Linux WebKit is not macOS/iOS Safari or a physical iPhone. Those systems, real screen locks, external appointments and clinical outcomes need separate verification.

Astro 7 and Tailwind 4 replace the older vulnerable build stack. Locale templates are generated by `scripts/localized-routes.mjs` using the filesystem, without a glob-parser dependency. The ignored template copies are removed after a build. No Google Fonts request is made. Health reflection pages do not include Cloudflare analytics; home/about retain the existing page analytics. Hosting/CDN infrastructure can still process requests. No answers, notes or scores belong in a URL, telemetry event, title, console output or bug report.

Volume edits during audio fades must preserve both the live gain and Howler's stored playback volume for the next loop. `patches/howler@2.2.4.patch` fixes the pinned dependency's public setter: stop the old fade before storing the requested value, and cancel native gain automation even when its bookkeeping interval has already finished. A dependency update must recheck this patch and the browser regressions. Chromium tests cover edits during a stretched fade and deliberately early bookkeeping completion; stored volume alone cannot prove the audible gain stayed correct. Reduced-motion transitions remain zero on the soundscape page as well as other routes.

## Deploy, inspect and roll back

1. Confirm the PR stays within the documented general-support/symptom-reference scope and run the full validation workflow on the final commit. Exact form and rights admission, age handling, safety support and private-data behavior are required. Independent clinical/native review must remain honestly uncompleted; do not broaden this edition into diagnostic or treatment claims.
2. On an approved merge to `main`, the existing GitHub Pages workflow deploys the exact artifact produced by its validation job. Do not deploy a separate local rebuild.
3. Check the public `/build-metadata.json` commit against the deployed commit. Smoke-test home → check-in → one activity → support, the crisis directory and language switching on the real domain. Confirm the privacy page can delete current and legacy browser records. Do not enter real health information into release tests.
4. Monitor failed deploys, broken internal routes and external service links. Recheck sources and service entry points when their providers change; record a real check date. Do not claim a provider is available or an appointment is booked from a click count.
5. To roll back, revert the release commit through a reviewed PR and let the same validated deployment workflow run. Saved notes are versioned; a rollback does not delete a user's downloads or device data. Keep the privacy deletion path available.
